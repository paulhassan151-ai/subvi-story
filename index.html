THE JOURNEY

How SUB^VI Changed

01

The Friendly Boy

SUB^VI was once friendly, funny, caring and full of life. His smile could make the people around him smile too.

He loved being around people and believed that life was meant to be shared with the people he loved.

02

The Bond

His mother was more than a parent. She was his safe place, his greatest supporter and his best friend.

Whenever the world became difficult, he knew he could always return to her.

03

The Loss

Then came the day that changed everything.

He lost his mother.

And with her death, it felt like a part of him disappeared too.

The Change

The laughter became quieter. The conversations became shorter.

SUB^VI slowly became distant.

People saw a cold boy. They didn't see the pain underneath.

Lingering Trauma

Time continued moving forward, but his memories stayed behind.

Some nights he still remembers her voice. Her smile. Her advice.

He doesn't talk about it. But he still carries it.

A LETTER I WISH YOU COULD READ

Dear Mum...

I don't know if you can hear me anymore, but there are so many things I wish I could tell you.

I miss your voice. I miss your smile. I miss knowing that no matter how difficult life became, I still had you.

People say I changed after you left. Maybe they're right.

I became quieter. I stopped trusting people with my heart. I learned how to smile even when I wasn't okay.

But I want you to know something...

I'm still trying.

And every good thing I become, I hope somehow you can see it.

Your son,
